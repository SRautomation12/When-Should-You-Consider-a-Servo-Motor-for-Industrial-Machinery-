# When-Should-You-Consider-a-Servo-Motor-for-Industrial-Machinery-
Does Your Machine Need More Accurate Movement?
Some tasks call for moving to exact positions, holding controlled speeds or repeating a motion precisely. A conventional motor setup may fall short of that.
How Does a Servo Motor Help?
A servo motor works with a suitable drive and a feedback system to control movement from programmed commands. Depending on the application, it gives accurate positioning, controlled speed and repeatable motion.
How Do You Pick the Right One?
Check the required torque, speed, load, movement profile and duty cycle, plus compatibility with your controller and drive. Correct sizing decides whether you get the performance you expect.
